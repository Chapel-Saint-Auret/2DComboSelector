## Final Recommendation

Final practical recommendation assigned to each combination after considering both overall ranking performance (final consensus rank)

and feasibility-related criteria (complexity, compatibility, and peak detection rate).

This field is intended as the final decision-oriented output of the workflow.

**Table 1:** Final recommendation levels and corresponding decision thresholds applied in our workflow.

| Recommendation | Final rank | Peak detection rate | Compatibility | Complexity | Rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Highly recommended | Top 10% | > 80% | Not Low | Not High | AND |
| Not recommended | Bottom 40% | < 40% | — | — | OR |
| Recommended | Top 30% | > 60% | Not Low | Not High | AND |
| Use with caution | All others | — | — | — | — |

*The recommendation rules were applied hierarchically: combinations satisfying the highly recommended criteria were assigned first, followed by not recommended, then recommended. All remaining combinations were classified as use with caution.*
